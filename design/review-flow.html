What the app implements
  • Request after 10 different completed pictures, then 20, 30… Repainting the same picture does not advance the count.
  • Only request after returning to the gallery, with the app active. The store may suppress the dialog.
  • No extra opinion question, suggested five-star score, incentive or custom rating overlay.
  • The completion count stays on the device. Analytics is deferred to a later update.
