# Privacy Policy for Rainbow Paint by Numbers

**Last updated:** June 27, 2026

Rainbow Paint by Numbers is a children's paint-by-numbers mobile app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## Summary

Rainbow Paint by Numbers does not collect, sell, share, rent, or transmit personal information to the developer.

The app does not include:

- User accounts or sign-in
- Advertising or third-party ad networks
- Analytics SDKs
- In-app purchases
- Developer-operated servers
- Cross-app or cross-site tracking
- Location tracking
- Camera or photo library uploads
- Microphone recording or audio uploads

## Information Stored on Your Device

Rainbow Paint by Numbers stores gameplay information locally on your device so the app can remember progress between sessions. This may include:

- Completed and in-progress levels
- Painting progress percentages
- Painted region data for unfinished pictures
- Locally generated preview images for in-progress pictures
- Review prompt state, such as whether the review prompt has already been shown or selected

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded to the developer, shared with third parties, or linked to your identity by the developer.

## App Store Review Prompt

The app may ask whether you would like to leave a review on the App Store after enough levels have been completed. If you choose to leave a review, Apple handles the review submission process. Rainbow Paint by Numbers does not receive personal information from that process.

## Children's Privacy

Rainbow Paint by Numbers is designed for children and families. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer in connection with this app, please contact us using the support information provided on the App Store listing.

## Data Deletion

Because gameplay data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to the developer, the developer cannot access or delete it remotely.

You can delete local app data by deleting the app from your device. You can also reset individual pictures inside the app.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's privacy practices change. Any updates will be posted in this file with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For privacy questions about Rainbow Paint by Numbers, please use the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
